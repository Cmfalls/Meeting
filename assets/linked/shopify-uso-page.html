The USO's Reach

Wherever Troops Serve, the USO Is There.

The USO operates in 55 U.S. states and territories and across 134 countries, reaching service members in combat zones, isolated postings, and everywhere in between. They've been doing it since 1941.

Your donation goes directly to USO programs: care packages, entertainment tours, transition support, family resources, and more. It's support that reaches troops wherever they are.
