From Flags USA · May 13, 2026

An Announcement from Flags USA

We're a proud partner of the USO.

After 38 years of making American flags, we're standing alongside an organization whose mission lines up with everything we believe in. Today we're announcing a partnership with the USO, the leading nonprofit supporting active-duty troops and their families. Through December 2026, every Flags USA order helps fund the programs and services service members rely on.

How You Give

At checkout, you'll see an option to add a $2.50 donation to your order. The full amount goes directly to USO programs. No fee, no markup, no fine print.

We're also matching every dollar contributed through the campaign, up to $25,000. Your $2.50 turns into $5.00 in support.

Why This Partnership

The USO has stood beside service members since 1941. They operate in 55 U.S. states and territories and across 134 countries, reaching troops in combat zones, isolated postings, and everywhere in between.

For us, a flag has always represented more than fabric and grommets. The flag stands for the people who serve and the families who serve alongside them. Partnering with the USO lets every Flags USA order mean a little more.

"At Flags USA, the American flag has always represented far more than a product. The flag represents the people who have sacrificed to protect the freedoms we all share."

Kim Dacka · CEO, Flags USA

What's Ahead

The campaign runs through December 2026, anchored to America's 250th birthday year. We'll share updates as the match builds and as the impact grows.

For now, the easiest way to support is at checkout. Add the $2.50 on your next order. We'll handle the rest.
